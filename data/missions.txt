# Athas mission definitions.
#
# Missions are the guide rails of progression: each one watches a single
# counter, shows its progress in the Missions screen, and pays out when
# you press Claim. Progress updates automatically where things actually
# happen (math rounds solved, artifacts found, level-ups, skill
# upgrades) - no bookkeeping by hand.
#
# Fields:
#   type:    what to count - math_correct | artifacts | level | upgrades
#   target:  progress needed to complete the mission
#   reward_points / reward_xp / reward_artifact_points: paid on claim
